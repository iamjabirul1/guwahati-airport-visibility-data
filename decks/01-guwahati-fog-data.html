Fog codes cluster in two months

  • METAR FG appears in 382 January reports, 156 December reports and 29 February reports across the series.
  • Every other month is in single digits.
  • December 1 to February 15 before 10:00: 79.8% of reports were below 1.5 mi (4,029 of 5,052).

What this is not

  • Reported visibility is not a delay, cancellation or runway record.
  • A 2,000 m report with mist is routine here; CAT III aircraft and trained crews operate well below 1.5 km.
  • Read the numbers as how often the airport looks like this, not how often your flight is late.

What we do with it

  • December to mid-February, a pre-dawn departure is planned on the airport side of the city.
  • Booking advice: an afternoon arrival, or a buffer day, if you are flying in on a festival morning.
  • The free Northeast trip planner uses the same data for its fog warning.

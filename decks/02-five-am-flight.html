Where to stay for a 5 AM flight from Guwahati

The airport is at Borjhar on the western edge. Most of the city is on the other side of it.

The one decision that matters

  • Stay on the airport side, not in the city centre.
  • Crossing Guwahati to reach LGBI is a long drive before dawn, and pre-dawn cab availability is the weak point, not the drive itself.

What to check before you book

  • 24-hour front desk. Some small properties lock up at night; a 3:45 AM checkout should not be a negotiation.
  • How far the terminal really is, measured, not described.
  • Whether the property arranges a car, or whether you are hunting for one in the dark.
  • Reliable Wi-Fi for web check-in the evening before.

A routine that works

  • Check in online the night before.
  • Settle the bill before you sleep.
  • Keep ID and bags together by the door.
  • Ask reception for a wake-up knock as a backup to your alarm.

When the city is fine

  • If your flight is after 9 AM, none of this applies.
  • Leaving the city at 6:30 for a mid-morning flight is normal.

Shillong Cherry Blossom Festival, 20-21 November 2026

Two days in Shillong, one decision at the start: go straight there, or sleep near the airport first.

Land, then decide

  • Land before early afternoon and you can drive straight to Shillong.
  • Land in the evening and the hill road at night is the wrong first impression of Meghalaya.

The road

  • Guwahati to Shillong is about 100 km, roughly 3 to 3.5 hours.
  • Ask the driver for the Jorabat bypass rather than the city route.
  • Umiam Lake is the natural tea stop.

The night-before option

  • Sleep on the airport side, leave at first light, and arrive in daylight with the festival ahead of you.
  • Free luggage storage means you can leave bags behind if your trip continues.

Festival weekend logistics

  • Shillong rooms sell out for the festival dates; Guwahati is the overflow plan.
  • Pre-book the intercity car; shared sumos leave only when full.
  • Keep cash for the road.

Guwahati to Shillong: route, timing and stops

About 100 km, and the difference between a good drive and a bad one is where you start.

The numbers

  • Roughly 100 km between Guwahati and Shillong.
  • About 3 to 3.5 hours in normal conditions.
  • NH6 climbs through Nongpoh and past Umiam Lake.

Route choice

  • Ask for the Jorabat bypass. It skips the worst of the city traffic.
  • From the airport at Borjhar you are already west of the jam, which saves 30 to 45 minutes over a city-centre start.

When to leave

  • Morning departure means daylight for the whole climb.
  • Hill roads at night, especially in monsoon fog, are tiring and slow.
  • If you land after mid-afternoon, take the airport-side night and leave at 7 AM.

Cost and car

  • Agree per-car or per-seat before you get in.
  • Pre-book for the morning; app cabs rarely accept intercity trips.
  • Keep cash: ATMs thin out on the road.

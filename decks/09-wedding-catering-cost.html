What wedding catering costs per plate in Guwahati

There is no single number, because the same rupees buy very different plates. Here is what actually moves the price.

The four things that move a plate price

  • Veg or non-veg, which is the biggest single swing.
  • How many starters, mains and desserts are actually in the menu.
  • Live counters and mocktail bars, which add cost quickly.
  • The guaranteed headcount: a bigger commitment usually lowers the rate.

Menu components to compare line by line

  • Welcome drink, starters, mains, rice or breads, dessert.
  • Serving staff, and how many per fifty guests.
  • Whether hall rent is separate.
  • Whether the quoted price includes taxes.

Questions that settle a quote

  • What is the minimum guaranteed headcount, and when does it lock?
  • What is the advance, and what happens if the date moves?
  • Can we bring our own decorator, and is there a fee?
  • Can we taste before we commit?

Plan the total, not the plate

  • Take the plate rate times your realistic headcount, then add 10 to 15 percent for overruns.
  • A 150-name invite list often means 80 to 100 people in the room at any one time.

Booking a 100-guest banquet hall in Guwahati

Most halls are built for 300. Here is the checklist for a 100-person event, in the order that saves money.

Check the layout, not the number

  • Ask for seated capacity separately from floating capacity.
  • Layouts change everything: round tables seat 70, cluster tables 80, theatre 100.
  • Visit at the same hour as your event.

Before you pay an advance

  • Is the hall rent separate from catering, and does a package reduce it?
  • Are there rooms on site, so outstation relatives do not need a second hotel?
  • Is parking inside the gate, and for how many cars?
  • Outside decorators: allowed, and at what fee?
  • Generator backup, and the cancellation terms.

Plan for the peak, not the invite list

  • Guests arrive in waves. A 150-invite evening reception often has 80 to 100 people in the room at once.
  • Cater for the peak and let the tail settle.

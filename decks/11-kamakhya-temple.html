Kamakhya Temple: where to stay, by arrival mode

Three realistic choices, and the right one depends on how you arrive, not on the star rating.

Option 1: Nilachal Hill lodges

  • Walking distance from the temple.
  • Basic, lively during festivals, and not always bookable online.
  • Best for very early darshan.

Option 2: City centre

  • Paltan Bazaar, Ulubari, Dispur side.
  • The right base if you arrive by train, because the station is close.
  • Temple is roughly a 25 to 40 minute drive depending on traffic.
hotelshinygrand.online/blog/kamakhya-temple-visit-guide

Option 3: Airport side

  • The sensible base if you fly in or out the same day.
  • Choose it if you would rather not do a long city transfer after landing.
hotelshinygrand.online/blog/kamakhya-temple-visit-guide

Practical points

  • Go before 7 AM; queues and traffic both build after that.
  • Dress modestly, carry little, security is tight.
  • During Ambubachi Mela in June expect crowd control and road closures; book a month ahead.
  • Hire a driver for the day if you want Umananda and Navagraha too.

An 8-hour layover at Guwahati airport

What is actually possible once you account for immigration, baggage and the margin you should not spend.

By the clock

  • Under 3 hours: stay inside the terminal.
  • 3 to 6 hours: a room near the airport beats airport chairs. Shower, meal, real nap, back in minutes.
  • 6 to 10 hours, daytime: Kamakhya Temple or the riverfront is doable with a booked car.
  • Overnight: book near the airport rather than in the city.

Return margin

  • Leave at least 3 hours before departure for your return to the terminal.
  • Guwahati traffic is unpredictable and the airport road has few alternatives.

If you leave the airport

  • Collect baggage before you exit, especially on separate tickets.
  • Agree waiting time with your driver up front.
  • Carry small cash; cards work in cafes, not at every stall.

Hotel Shiny Grand

  • 1.7 km from Terminal 2, about 5 minutes by road.
  • Normal check-in 12:00, check-out 11:00; early check-in is chargeable and subject to availability.
  • Free luggage storage, and late checkout up to 3 hours free.
  • 24/7 front desk, so an odd-hour arrival is not a problem.

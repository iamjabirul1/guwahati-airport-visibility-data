Guwahati airport transfer: prepaid, app cab or hotel pickup

By day all three work. At night they stop being equivalent.

The prepaid counter

  • Fixed fare by distance, no surge, receipt with the car number.
  • Queue risk when several flights land together.
  • The safer choice after about 10 PM.

App cabs

  • Often cheaper in the daytime.
  • Pickup is at a designated zone, not the arrivals kerb.
  • Late-night supply gets patchy, especially toward the city centre.

Hotel pickup

  • The driver knows your name and your hotel knows your flight.
  • That matters most after a delayed arrival, when you are the last person at the gate.

Questions before you get in

  • Is the fare for the whole car or per person?
  • Are tolls and parking inside the fare?
  • Going to Shillong? Do not take an app cab; book an intercity car.
  • Note the car number and share it with someone.

Hotel Shiny Grand

  • Direct bookings include one complimentary one-way airport transfer, pickup or drop.
  • The transfer is one way only, so choose the leg you value most.
  • 1.7 km from Terminal 2, about 450 m from Terminal 1.

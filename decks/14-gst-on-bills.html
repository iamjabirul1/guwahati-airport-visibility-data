GST on hotel rooms and banquet bills in India

The tax line is where quotes stop being comparable. Ask one question and the numbers line up.

The single question

  • Ask whether the quoted price includes taxes.
  • A ₹3,000 room and a ₹3,000 room are different rooms if one is plus tax.

Rooms

  • A GST rate applies on top of the room tariff at this hotel: 5%.
  • Rooms are quoted per night.
  • Ask for the tax-inclusive total for your dates before you compare two hotels.

Food and catering

  • Catering and restaurant bills carry their own tax treatment, and menus may be quoted either way.
  • For a wedding, get the per-plate price and the hall rent on the same page, each marked with its tax status.

Why it matters at 100 guests

  • On a hundred plates, a 5% difference is a real number.
  • The compare-at-a-glance method: plate rate times peak headcount, plus hall rent, plus tax, plus 10 to 15 percent overrun.

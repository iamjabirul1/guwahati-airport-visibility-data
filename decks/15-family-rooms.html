Family rooms in Guwahati: what three people actually get

Room categories sound similar online and behave very differently when you are three in one room.

Read the occupancy, not the name

  • A deluxe room and a triple room are not interchangeable.
  • Some rates assume single or double occupancy; the third guest changes the rate.

The questions that matter

  • How many beds, and what size, for three people?
  • Is the extra guest charged separately?
  • Is breakfast included for everyone in the room?
  • Is there space for a fourth, if plans change?

Best time to visit Guwahati and the Northeast

Month by month, and the one month that needs a plan rather than a warning.

October to March: the easy window

  • Cooler days, clearer skies, festival season.
  • Also the busiest, so book festival weekends first.

December to mid-February: the fog window

  • At LGBI Airport, 49.7% of reports in these months showed visibility below 1.5 miles (2.4 km).
  • Low visibility clusters between 4 AM and 9 AM.
  • Pre-dawn departures need a buffer, an airport-side night, or both.
hotelshinygrand.online/blog/best-time-to-visit-guwahati

April to May: hot, before the rain

  • Daytime heat builds through May.
  • Fewer crowds; do the hill stations rather than the plains.
hotelshinygrand.online/blog/best-time-to-visit-guwahati

June to September: monsoon

  • Green and dramatic, with rain on most days.
  • Roads in the hills slow down; waterfalls and Kaziranga's rivers are at their fullest.
  • Build slack into any itinerary with a fixed flight at the end.

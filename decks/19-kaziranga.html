Kaziranga from Guwahati: how to plan it

A long drive each way, two nights minimum, and a park that is closed for part of the year.

Plan it as two nights, not a day trip

  • The drive from Guwahati is long enough that a same-day return wastes the trip.
  • Two nights gives you two safari slots and a rest.

When to go

  • The park closes for the monsoon months, so check the season before you book rooms.
  • Peak season is busy; book jeep safaris early.
  • Winters are cool and clear, with the best visibility for sightings.

Make the drive part of the plan

  • Leave early; you will want daylight at both ends.
  • Keep the last night before your flight on the airport side.
  • Free luggage storage at the hotel means you can leave extra bags behind.

The pattern that works

  • Night 1: airport side, after your flight.
  • Nights 2-3: Kaziranga.
  • Last night: airport side again, then your flight.
